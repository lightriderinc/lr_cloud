## 1. What Light Rider Quantum provides

- **Job submission.** You can submit quantum circuits to run on simulators and on real QPUs operated by third parties (for example, IQM, Rigetti and IBM Quantum), then view each job's status and results.
- **Backend information.** We show information about available backends, such as qubit counts, topology, calibration data and live status, as reported by the hardware providers.
- **Reservations.** On supported hardware, you can book an exclusive block of QPU time.
- **Managed services and applications.** We may offer managed quantum services (such as quantum error correction workflows) and demonstration applications built on quantum hardware or entropy.
- **Classroom.** Interactive learning content, including circuits you can run on a simulator.
- **API and SDK.** Programmatic access to submit jobs and retrieve results.

Which backends, services and features are available can change at any time, including because a hardware provider adds, retires, recalibrates or takes a device offline.

## 2. Credits and pricing

- **Simulators.** Simulator and mock backends that we label as free do not consume Credits.
- **QPU jobs.** Jobs on real QPUs consume Credits at the rate shown in Light Rider Quantum when you submit the job (for example, a price per shot). The Credits for a job are charged when it is accepted for submission.
- **Purchase required for real hardware.** Promotional Credits (such as a sign-up bonus) can be used on simulators but **cannot be used on real QPUs or for reservations until your account has made a qualifying purchase or received Credits from another user**, as shown in Light Rider Quantum.
- **Failed and cancelled jobs.** If a job cannot be run because of a fault on our side or the hardware provider's, we will return the Credits charged for it. Credits are not returned for jobs that run but produce results you do not want, that fail because of an error in your circuit or parameters, or that you cancel after execution has started.

## 3. Reservations

- A reservation gives you exclusive access to the named device for the booked time slot. The price is shown before you confirm and may include a Light Rider service fee on top of the hardware provider's own price. **Credits for a reservation are charged when the booking is confirmed.**
- **Unless stated otherwise when you book, reservations cannot be cancelled or rescheduled by you and are not refundable, including if you do not use the reserved time.**
- If the hardware provider cancels your reservation, or the device is unavailable for a material part of the slot because of a provider outage, we will return the Credits for the affected time or, where the provider allows, help you rebook. This is your only remedy for a provider cancellation or outage.
- Reservation availability, slot lengths and prices are set by the hardware providers and may change.
