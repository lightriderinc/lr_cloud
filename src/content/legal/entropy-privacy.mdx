This Privacy Notice gives more detail about the personal information that **Light Rider Entropy** handles. It supplements the Light Rider [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy), which covers your Light Rider Cloud account and every Light Rider platform. Read the two together. If you have questions, contact [info@lightriderinc.com](mailto:info@lightriderinc.com).

> **We do not keep the random bytes we deliver to you.** They are never saved to our databases, which have no place to hold them, and receipts do not include them.

## 1. Information Light Rider Entropy handles

| Information | Details | Why we use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draw records | Your account identifier, the mode and Sources requested, number of bytes, cost in Credits, status, any failure reason, whether the request came from the website or the API, the API key prefix used, and timestamps | To deliver and bill draws, return Credits for failed draws, show your history, provide support and prevent abuse |
| Signed receipts | The receipt for each successful draw, including the Sources that contributed, the amount delivered, health-test results, a commitment, the signing key's fingerprint and timestamps, linked to your account | So you can view, download and verify receipts, and for audit and dispute resolution |
| Billing records | Your email address and the draw details needed to charge your Credits, shared with Light Rider Cloud, which runs the shared wallet | To charge and refund Credits |
| Browser session history | Your most recent draws, including the bytes, kept only in your browser tab's session storage | So you can look back at draws during that session. It is deleted when you close the tab and is never sent to us. |
| Interface preferences | Your chosen mode, view and documentation language, stored in your browser | To remember your preferences on that device |

Account, billing, credit and log information is described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 2. Entropy sources

Our entropy service requests raw randomness from Sources, some of which are run by third parties (for example, ANU, Cisco Outshift, QiSpace, IQM, Rigetti and public randomness beacons). These requests are made by Light Rider's systems, under Light Rider's credentials. **We do not send your name, email address or account identifier to Source operators.**

## 3. Retention

- **Generated random bytes:** not saved to our databases or receipts.
- **Draw records:** kept while your account is open and afterwards for as long as needed for billing, audit and dispute purposes, as described in the Privacy Policy.
- **Signed receipts:** receipts are designed as a permanent, verifiable record and are kept for as long as they may need to be verified, which may continue after your account is closed. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove or de-identify the link between your receipts and your personal information where we can, and keep receipts only for verification, audit and legal purposes.
- **Browser data:** session history is deleted when you close the tab; other preferences stay until you clear your browser storage.

## 4. Your choices

You can download your receipts at any time, rotate or revoke your API key in your settings, and exercise the rights described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) by emailing [info@lightriderinc.com](mailto:info@lightriderinc.com).
