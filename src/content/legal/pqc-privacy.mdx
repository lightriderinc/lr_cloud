This Privacy Notice gives more detail about the personal information that **Light Rider PQC** handles. It supplements the Light Rider [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy), which covers your Light Rider Cloud account and every Light Rider platform. Read the two together. If you have questions, contact [info@lightriderinc.com](mailto:info@lightriderinc.com).

> **In short:** files you upload to qSearch are deleted as soon as the scan finishes, and the scan results go back to you without being stored. The PQC applications run in your browser, and what you type into them is not sent to us.

## 1. qSearch uploads and Findings

| Information | What happens to it |
| --- | --- |
| Files you upload | Written to temporary storage on our server, scanned, and **deleted as soon as the scan finishes**, whether it succeeds or fails. They are not written to a database. |
| Findings | Returned to your browser. **We do not store them.** If you download Findings, the file is created in your browser. |
| Scan metadata | Our servers may record operational information such as when a scan ran, its size, whether it succeeded and any error, in server logs. These logs do not contain the contents of your files. |

qSearch requires you to be signed in, so scans are associated with your account session while they run.

Your uploads may contain personal information, for example names or email addresses in code comments, or credentials. We process it only to perform the scan, and it is deleted with the file. Please remove information you do not need us to process.

## 2. PQC applications

- **Processed on your device.** Keys, messages, files, secrets, secret shares and tokens that you enter into the Applications are processed in your browser and **are not sent to Light Rider**.
- **Entropy requests.** When an Application asks for Light Rider entropy (for example, to seed key generation), your browser requests random bytes from our servers. Our servers obtain them from our entropy sources, which may include public randomness beacons such as NIST's or Light Rider's quantum backend, and send them to your browser. We do not save the bytes to a database. The request does not send your keys or other Application inputs to us.
- **Browser storage.** Speed-test results, together with basic device information used to interpret them (your device's platform and number of processor cores), are kept in your browser's session storage until you close the tab. They are not sent to us.
- **Downloads.** Keys and other files you download are created in your browser and saved to your device. We do not receive a copy.

## 3. Account and cookies

Light Rider PQC uses your Light Rider account and single sign-on, including the sign-in session and single sign-on helper cookies described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). It also lets you manage your profile, including your profile picture, which is stored at a public web address.

## 4. Retention

- **Uploaded files:** deleted immediately after the scan.
- **Findings:** not stored by us.
- **Application inputs and outputs:** stay on your device.
- **Server logs:** generally kept for no longer than 12 months.

## 5. Your choices

You can choose not to upload files you are not comfortable having scanned, use test keys and tokens in the Applications, and clear your browser storage at any time. To exercise the rights described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy), email [info@lightriderinc.com](mailto:info@lightriderinc.com).
