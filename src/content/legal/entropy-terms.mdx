## 1. What Light Rider Entropy provides

- **Random bytes.** You can draw random bytes ("**Entropy**") from a preset pool, from a custom pool of sources you choose, or from a single source, in the amounts allowed by Light Rider Entropy.
- **Sources.** Entropy is drawn from quantum random number generators, quantum processors and other hardware or public randomness sources, some of which are operated by third parties (each a "**Source**"). The catalogue shows which Sources are currently available. Sources marked "Coming soon" are not available.
- **Processing.** Raw output from Sources is health-tested and cryptographically conditioned and mixed before delivery, as described in our documentation.
- **Signed receipts.** Each successful draw produces a receipt describing it (for example, the Sources that contributed, the amount delivered, health-test outcomes and a commitment), signed with a Light Rider signing key. You can verify a receipt using our published public key.

## 4. What "certified" and "verified" mean

Light Rider Entropy describes Entropy as certified or verified. **This means that each batch is subjected to statistical health tests (including tests modeled on NIST SP 800-90B) and that each successful draw comes with a cryptographically signed receipt.** Unless we expressly state otherwise in writing for a specific Source or product, it does **not** mean that Light Rider Entropy, any Source, or any output has been certified, validated or approved by NIST or any other government body, accreditation body or independent laboratory, including under the Entropy Source Validation or Cryptographic Module Validation Programs.

Health tests reduce, but do not eliminate, the risk of defective output. A receipt shows how a draw was produced and that the receipt has not been altered; it is not a guarantee that the output is unpredictable.

We may rotate our receipt signing keys. Receipts signed with an earlier key remain verifiable with that key, which we will continue to publish for as long as reasonably practicable.
