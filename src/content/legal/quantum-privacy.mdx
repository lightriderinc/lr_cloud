This Privacy Notice gives more detail about the personal information that **Light Rider Quantum** handles. It supplements the Light Rider [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy), which covers your Light Rider Cloud account and every Light Rider platform. Read the two together. If you have questions, contact [info@lightriderinc.com](mailto:info@lightriderinc.com).

## 1. Information Light Rider Quantum handles

| Information | Details | Why we use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job records | Job identifier, the backend you chose, number of shots, status, cost in Credits, and when the job was created and finished | To run your jobs, show your job history, charge Credits and provide support |
| Circuits and results | The circuit and parameters you submit, and the measurement results returned | To execute your job and show you its results |
| Reservations | Device, start and end time, price, the service fee applied, Credits charged, any notes you add, and the provider's reservation reference | To book, manage and bill your reservation and to resolve issues with the provider |
| API usage | Your API key prefix, when it was last used, request counts for rate limiting, and usage events for metered plans | To authenticate and meter API requests and prevent abuse |
| Interface preferences | Whether you have seen product tours or welcome messages, your recently run classroom experiments, and backend list view settings, stored in your browser | To remember your preferences on that device |

Account, billing, credit and log information is described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 2. Hardware providers

To execute a job or reservation, we send the technical content needed (the circuit, target device, number of shots and similar parameters) to the third-party operator of that device, such as **IQM**, **Rigetti** or **IBM Quantum**. Requests go from Light Rider's own job service, using Light Rider's credentials. **We do not send your name, email address or account identifier to hardware providers.** Providers may keep technical logs of the jobs they run under their own privacy policies, so avoid putting personal or confidential information in circuit names, labels or metadata.

Backend status and calibration information that we display comes from the providers and does not contain information about you.

## 3. Retention

- Job records and reservation records are kept while your account is open, so you can see your history, and for as long as afterwards as we need them for billing, audit and dispute purposes, as described in the Privacy Policy.
- Circuits and results are kept by our job service so you can retrieve them, and may be removed after a period of inactivity or when your account is closed. Download results you want to keep.
- Information stored in your browser stays there until you clear it.

## 4. Your choices

You can view your jobs and reservations in Light Rider Quantum, rotate or revoke your API key in your settings, and exercise the rights described in the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) by emailing [info@lightriderinc.com](mailto:info@lightriderinc.com).
