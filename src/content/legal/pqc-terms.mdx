## 1. What Light Rider PQC provides

- **qSearch.** You can upload a source file, configuration file, certificate or ZIP archive, and qSearch scans it for cryptography that is vulnerable to quantum attacks (the "**Findings**").
- **Applications.** Tools and demonstrations that run in your browser, such as encryption and signing with ML-KEM and ML-DSA, secret splitting, a JWT inspector, a PQC speed test, a compatibility check against published test vectors, and a harvest-now-decrypt-later demonstration (the "**Applications**").
- **Entropy for key generation.** Some Applications can seed key generation with randomness supplied by Light Rider's entropy service.

- **You must have the right to upload what you scan.** Only upload code and files that you own or are authorized to analyze, and make sure doing so does not breach any confidentiality obligation, license or law.
